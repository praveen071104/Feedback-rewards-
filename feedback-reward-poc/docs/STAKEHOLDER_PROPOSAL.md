# Stakeholder Proposal: M&S Customer Feedback Experience

**Purpose:** Present a proposed refinement to the customer feedback experience and agree the next steps for evaluating it.

## Executive Summary

The current feedback journey is clear and accessible, but some wording feels informal and promotional. This proposal introduces a more professional, customer-centred tone that better reflects M&S while explaining why feedback matters, what makes it useful and what customers can expect after submitting it.

The change is a focused content update. It does not change the form fields, feedback processing, ticket workflow or reward logic. In this proof of concept, rewards remain illustrative and are not issued.

## Problem Statement

Customers are asked to share feedback that may inform product, colleague and store improvements. The current wording does not consistently communicate that purpose: phrases such as "Tell us how we did" and "Feedback that earns you more" can sound casual or primarily promotional. Reward wording may also create an expectation that a reward is guaranteed, when eligibility is conditional.

This creates an opportunity to strengthen trust and brand alignment, set clearer expectations, and encourage feedback with enough detail to support review and action.

## Proposed Solution

Refine the customer-facing copy without changing the underlying workflow. The revised experience will:

- Lead with the value of customer feedback and its role in improving the M&S experience.
- Use professional, direct labels and instructions throughout the form.
- Prompt for relevant details, including product, colleague, location and date where applicable.
- Describe Sparks rewards as conditional on eligibility rather than guaranteed.
- Explain that submissions are acknowledged immediately and may be referred for further review.

### Proposed Customer-Facing Copy

| Experience area | Proposed wording |
| --- | --- |
| Main heading | Help Us Deliver an Exceptional Experience |
| Introduction | We value your feedback and use it to improve every aspect of the M&S experience. Share your thoughts about a product, colleague or store visit, and help us continue raising our standards. Detailed compliments and minor complaints may qualify for a reward, subject to colleague approval. |
| Form heading | Submit Your Feedback |
| Form instruction | This form can be completed in under a minute. Fields marked * are mandatory. |
| Store field | Select Store |
| Rating field | How would you rate your experience? |
| Feedback prompt | Please provide details of your experience, including the relevant product, colleague, location and date where applicable. |
| Reward panel | Rewarding Meaningful Feedback. Detailed compliments and minor complaints may qualify for a Sparks reward, subject to colleague approval. Specific feedback about a colleague, product, service or experience is especially valuable. Where improvements are needed, we will review your feedback carefully and take appropriate action. |
| Next steps panel | Our Review Process. Your feedback will be acknowledged immediately. Where further review is required, it will be assessed by the appropriate team and handled in accordance with our customer service process. |

## Expected Benefits

- **Brand alignment:** A more considered tone supports a premium, professional M&S customer experience.
- **Clearer expectations:** Conditional reward language helps avoid implying that every submission receives a reward.
- **More actionable feedback:** The prompt calls out the details that can help teams investigate and respond.
- **Greater transparency:** Customers receive a clearer description of acknowledgement and follow-up.

These are intended outcomes, not measured results. They should be evaluated with customer and operational data before wider rollout.

## Reward Rules

- Detailed, genuine major compliments are recommended for a mid-tier Sparks reward.
- Genuine minor complaints receive an apology and are recommended for a low-tier Sparks reward, even when more detail is needed.
- The analyser suggests a tier, but a store colleague makes the final eligibility decision in Insights. Eligible decisions appear as issued records in Rewards; the app does not transfer money or update a Sparks balance.

## Scope and Dependencies

**In scope:** Customer-facing wording on the feedback form, including the introduction, form labels, reward panel and review-process panel.

**Out of scope:** Changes to submission fields, rating capture, category definitions, staff ticket handling, reward amounts, or reward issuance after approval.

Before production use, stakeholders should confirm the Sparks eligibility wording and reward policy, the team responsible for follow-up, and any required brand, legal, privacy and customer-service approvals. The current proof of concept explicitly states that no real rewards are issued; that disclaimer must remain until a production reward process is approved and implemented.

## Evaluation Approach

Establish a baseline, then compare the revised experience using measures such as:

- Feedback form completion and abandonment rates.
- Completeness and specificity of submitted feedback.
- The proportion of submissions that can be routed or investigated without follow-up for basic details.
- Customer understanding of whether a Sparks reward is conditional.
- Customer satisfaction with acknowledgement and follow-up.

Review results alongside qualitative feedback and operational capacity. No success thresholds are proposed until baseline data and measurement feasibility are confirmed.

## Stakeholder Decisions Requested

1. Approve the proposed professional, customer-centred tone for the proof-of-concept experience.
2. Confirm the approved wording for reward eligibility and the disclaimer required before production.
3. Confirm ownership and expected handling process for feedback requiring further review.
4. Agree which measures and baseline period should be used to evaluate the copy update.
